Scarwise

Your AI agents make a mistake once. Not twice. A local safety layer between your AI agents and their tools — Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Copilot, Kiro, Qwen Code, OpenCode, Claude.ai and ChatGPT. StayLit

Keeps your Windows screen from locking while the PC is idle. Zero setup: StayLit finds the lock time set by company policy, screen saver or power plan, and moves the mouse 1 pixel and back right before it — only when the PC is idle, never while you work. No admin rights, free.
