Parlanote

The vocabulary notebook that speaks.
For teachers, students and everyone learning a language.

A paper vocabulary notebook can't tell you how a word sounds or whether you spelled it right, and it is hard to share with a class. Parlanote is the notebook that does: write a new word or phrase, and it checks the spelling, fills in the translation and says it out loud — on your phone and on your PC, even without internet.

Why

  • Hear every word: a speaker button next to each word and its translation, with your device's voice.
  • Never learn it wrong: a typo is shown with the right spelling before you save the word.
  • Lists from the teacher: send today's words in any messenger; one tap adds them to every student's notebook.
  • Test yourself: hide one column, remember, tap the card to check; print a practice sheet.
  • Works offline, on Windows and Android, in 17 languages.
  • Private: no account, no ads, no data collection — your words stay on your device.

Install

From the latest release: ParlanoteSetup.exe for Windows 10 / 11, Parlanote.apk for Android 8.0 or later. The step-by-step guide is on GitHub.
