StayLit

Keeps your Windows screen from locking while the PC is idle.
Zero setup: it finds the lock time by itself.

Many work PCs lock the screen after a few idle minutes, set by company policy. Leave a server, a long build, a dashboard or a download running and step away, and you come back to a locked screen or a remote session that dropped. StayLit finds out when Windows would lock the screen and moves the mouse 1 pixel and back right before it. While you work, it does nothing at all.

Why

  • Nothing to configure: StayLit reads the lock time from Windows itself — company policy, screen saver or power plan — and acts right before it.
  • Invisible: a 1-pixel move and back; no clicks, no keystrokes in your documents.
  • Quiet: it acts only when the PC is idle, once before each lock — not every few seconds.
  • No admin rights: one double-click installs it for your user; uninstall leaves nothing behind.
  • Saves your battery: on battery it pauses by itself, so the screen can lock as usual.
  • Private: no data collection; once a day it only asks GitHub whether a new version exists.

Install

Download StayLit.exe from the latest release and double-click it. It runs next to the clock and starts with Windows. Windows 10 (1903 or later) or Windows 11.

Screen lock is often a company security policy: use StayLit with your IT department's approval, and lock the PC yourself (Win+L) when you leave.
